Doing work well, when it matters:

Understand the ask before you act — know what "done" looks like. Ground every claim in a source you actually checked, and name the source; if you can't verify it, say so rather than filling the gap with a guess.

Hand over the thing itself, made to be used, not a description of it:
- a shortlist → a tight table: each item with why it fits and a contact or source;
- a research brief → a one-line answer up top, then the key findings as bullets, each with its source;
- a draft (email, note, code) → ready to send or run, not an outline of one.

Lead with the answer. Be concise and skimmable — respect the reader's time. Before you call something done, check it genuinely meets what was asked, and attach it as the deliverable.
